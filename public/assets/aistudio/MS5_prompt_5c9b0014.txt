Build a vertical YouTube Short as a Three.js web project that renders frame by frame to an MP4.

=== FILES I AM GIVING YOU ===
- MS5_audio.wav  (final mix: voices + music + sound effects, 56.43 s, 48 kHz)
- MS5.srt    (one cue per spoken line, timings match the audio exactly)
Use the audio as the master clock. Total length = 56.43 s. Do not change or re-time the audio.

=== OUTPUT ===
- 1080x1920 (9:16), 30 fps, 1693 frames (56.43 s x 30).
- MP4: H.264 High profile, VBR target 16 Mbps / max 20 Mbps, keyframe every 15 frames, yuv420p, AAC 48 kHz stereo 320 kbps, +faststart.
- Use the GPU encoder if available (h264_nvenc, h264_amf or h264_qsv), otherwise libx264.

=== PROJECT STRUCTURE ===
- index.html: one <canvas> 1080x1920. Load three.js locally (no CDN at render time) with an importmap. Use EffectComposer + UnrealBloomPass (strength ~0.5, threshold ~0.75) + OutputPass. ACES tone mapping.
- main.js: everything is DETERMINISTIC and driven by time t (seconds). Expose:
    window.DUR = 56.43; window.ready (Promise that resolves after fonts, textures and shaders are loaded);
    window.renderAt(t) draws exactly the frame at time t;
    window.frameJPEG(t) returns renderAt(t) as a JPEG data URL (quality 0.95).
  No Math.random() - use a seeded PRNG. No requestAnimationFrame timing in render mode. Same t = same picture every time.
- Composite in a final 2D canvas: draw the WebGL frame, then characters, then text/infographics, captions, vignette, grain.
- render.js (Node): start a tiny local HTTP server, open the page in headless Chrome/Edge with puppeteer-core
  (flags: --ignore-gpu-blocklist --enable-gpu --use-angle=d3d11 on Windows), call frameJPEG(i/30) for every frame,
  pipe the JPEGs to ffmpeg (image2pipe, mjpeg) and mux with the WAV. Log progress to render_log.txt.
- RENDER.bat: checks Node is installed, runs "npm install puppeteer-core ffmpeg-static" the first time, then "node render.js".
- ?play URL mode: live preview with the audio for checking.

=== LOOK AND STYLE ===
- Channel: Voice of Times. Series name on screen: "MONEY SECRETS #5".
- Every shot = 3 depth layers:
  1) BACK: a full 3D world (sky dome with gradient, fog, stars or dust particles, lights). Never a flat or empty background.
  2) MIDDLE: 3D objects that explain the idea (price tags, cart, vault, coins, charts, bars, timeline).
  3) FRONT: two REALISTIC 3D characters (see CHARACTERS below), standing in the 3D scene and lit by the same lights.
- The camera always moves slowly (dolly, crane or orbit) and every shot starts with a short punch-in (FOV 8% tighter, easing out over 0.25 s).
- RULE: something NEW on screen at least every 3 seconds (new shot, new 3D object, new big number or new text). Max shot length 2.8 s.
- 2-frame white flash on cuts in the first 6 seconds and on the big moments only.
- Colors: dark backgrounds, money green #22E07A, alert red #FF3B5C, highlight yellow #FFD54A, cyan #3CE0FF, orange #FF6A3A.
- Font: Poppins Bold (bundle the TTF locally). All big text has a thick black outline and soft shadow.
- Restrained glow: bloom only on bright lines and numbers, never on white backgrounds.
- Film grain as a STILL noise image over everything at ~7% overlay, plus a soft vignette.
- No real logos, no real people, no character names on screen.

=== SAFE ZONES (1080x1920) ===
- Headline / big numbers: y 250-700.
- Captions: centred at y ~905, Poppins Bold 74 px, uppercase, black outline. Show 3 words at a time, timed inside each SRT cue
  (split the cue text into 3-word chunks, duration proportional to chunk length). Colour by speaker: Narrator white, Man yellow #FFD54A, Woman teal #5CF2D0.
- Characters: from y ~900 down to ~1720.
- Keep everything out of the bottom 200 px and the right 120 px (YouTube buttons).

=== FACT TAG COMPONENT ===
Rounded black pill with a green "REAL" badge on the left and white text, 40 px. Pops in with a small overshoot.

=== CHARACTERS (realistic 3D, no names) ===
Two unnamed characters. Never show or say a name.
- THE MAN: realistic adult man, late 20s, wearing a yellow hoodie and a red cap. Voice lines 2 and 8. In this story he is the borrower.
- THE WOMAN: realistic adult woman, late 20s, wearing a teal sweater, hair in a ponytail. Voice lines 5 and 10. In this story she is the banker (smart blazer over the teal top is fine).
Files (I will put them in assets/characters/ - downloaded from Mixamo as FBX "With Skin", converted to GLB):
  man.glb, woman.glb                      (rigged realistic characters)
  Animations (FBX "Without Skin" -> GLB), one file per clip, for each character:
  man_idle.glb, man_talking.glb, man_surprised.glb, man_shrugging.glb, man_cheering.glb, man_walking.glb, man_looking.glb
  woman_idle.glb, woman_talking.glb, woman_pointing.glb, woman_walking.glb, woman_typing.glb (Mixamo "Typing"; if missing use woman_idle)
How to use them:
- Load with GLTFLoader, play clips with THREE.AnimationMixer. Drive the mixer with mixer.setTime(t - clipStart) so every frame is deterministic (never use a real clock).
- Crossfade between clips over 0.3 s by blending two actions' weights from t.
- While a character speaks (times from the SRT), play the "talking" clip, plus a small head nod. If the model has a jaw/mouth morph target, open it to the audio rhythm; if not, keep the camera medium or wide on speakers (no mouth close-ups).
- Characters cast soft shadows (shadow-mapped key light, PCFSoft). Use a warm key light, cool rim light and an environment map (RoomEnvironment) so skin and clothes look real.
- Characters stand on the floor, never float. Keep them inside y 900-1720 on screen and never in front of big text.
- If a .glb file is missing, show a neutral grey stand-in mannequin and log a warning (do not crash).
